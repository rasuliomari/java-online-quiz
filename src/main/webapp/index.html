ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A smarter way to assess knowledge

The UDOM Online Quiz System is a web-based academic assessment platform concept designed around the university environment.

The platform provides separate environments for students, teachers and administrators, allowing each role to perform its specific academic and management activities.

Get Started

Digital Assessment

Conduct quizzes online and provide students with immediate access to their performance.

Performance Tracking

Students can review their quiz history while teachers can monitor academic performance.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything you need for online assessment

Designed to provide a simple, reliable and attractive online quiz experience.

Online Quizzes

Students can access available quizzes and complete assessments directly through the web platform.

Timed Assessments

Teachers can configure quiz durations and students can monitor their remaining time.

Instant Results

Automatically calculate scores and display results after a quiz is submitted.

Student Management

Administrators can manage student accounts and monitor system users.

Teacher Tools

Teachers can create quizzes, add questions, manage assessments and review results.

Role-Based Access

Separate student, teacher and administrator environments protect access to system features.
